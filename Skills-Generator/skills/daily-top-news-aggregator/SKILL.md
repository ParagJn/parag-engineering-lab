# Daily Top News Aggregator

## Overview

This skill searches the internet to identify and compile the top 10 most significant news stories from today, sourcing from reputable news outlets and providing balanced coverage across different categories. Perfect for users who want a quick, comprehensive overview of current events without information overload.

**Keywords**: news, current events, daily briefing, top stories, journalism, breaking news, headlines

## Core Framework

### Source Prioritization
- Focus on established news organizations with strong credibility
- Balance between international, national, and regional coverage
- Include diverse perspectives from multiple outlets

### Content Curation
- Rank stories by impact, relevance, and widespread coverage
- Categorize news across politics, economics, technology, health, and world events
- Verify information accuracy across multiple sources

## Features

- Real-time search of current news sources
- Ranking algorithm based on story prominence and source reliability
- Balanced representation across news categories
- Duplicate story filtering and consolidation
- Source attribution for each news item

## Output Format

- Numbered list (1-10) of top news stories
- Each entry includes: headline, brief summary (2-3 sentences), primary source, and timestamp
- Stories organized by importance/impact level
- Clear categorization tags for each story type

## Instructions

- Search current news from the past 24 hours only
- Prioritize breaking news and high-impact stories
- Verify each story appears in multiple credible sources
- Provide neutral, factual summaries without editorial bias
- Include direct links to original articles when possible
- Focus on global significance and public interest value

## Constraints

- Only include news from today's date
- Limit to reputable, established news organizations
- Avoid opinion pieces, editorials, or unverified reports
- Maintain political and ideological neutrality
- Exclude gossip, entertainment news, or purely commercial content
- Respect copyright by providing summaries, not full article reproduction
