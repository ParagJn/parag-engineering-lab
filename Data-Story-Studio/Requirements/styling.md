Instead of producing a generic dashboard with rides, dates, and bar charts, the system might discover that weekday commuting peaks dominate demand, weekend patterns differ significantly, and a particular month shows an unexpected decline.
It could then:
- Open with a headline summarizing the most significant supported finding.
- Animate a timeline to reveal how demand changes throughout the year.
- Highlight morning and evening peaks on an hourly heatmap.
- Show which stations or user segments contribute most to the change.
- Let the user click the decline to inspect the underlying records and query.
- Explain possible reasons without claiming causation that the data cannot establish.
The result is not just an attractive dashboard. It is an interactive explanation of what the data means.
